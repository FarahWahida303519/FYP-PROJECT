FROM KEDAH, FOR GENERATIONS TO COME

A language inherited.
A responsibility shared.

Loghat Kami documents the Kedah Malay dialect through community participation and language expert review.

Every voice belongs

Words, meanings, sentence contexts and pronunciation help us understand the unique language passed down through generations.

This project focuses on text and audio collection, AI-assisted preliminary screening and final decisions by language experts.
